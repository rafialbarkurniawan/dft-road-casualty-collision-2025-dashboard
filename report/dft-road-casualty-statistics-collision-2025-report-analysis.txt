--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY ANALYTICAL LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Geographic scope: The dataset covers Great Britain, not Indonesia.
- Reporting scope: The dataset only includes accidents resulting in injury,
  reported to the police, and recorded in the STATS19 system—not all traffic
  incidents.
- Source platform: Data originates from GOV.UK / Department for Transport
  (DfT) Road Safety Open Data.
- Accident count vs. risk: Accident counts alone are not a direct measure of
  risk; measuring risk requires denominators such as traffic volume, distance
  travelled, or vehicle fleet size.
- Time horizon: One year of data is insufficient to conclude long-term trends.
- Correlation vs. causation: Relationships among weather, road type, and
  severity do not prove cause and effect.
- Severity adjustment methodology: Severity figures should be interpreted with
  awareness of the DfT severity adjustment methodology.
